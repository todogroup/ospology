# Framework V2.0 review notes

The [events website](https://todogroup.org/community/events/) supplies the shared OSPOlogy brand and event categories. [Issue #749](https://github.com/todogroup/ospology/issues/749) and its [working document](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1nxVn6eL05c0u0BQGHJT6-JxF7pEWAbAepQtUwJdOTMk/edit) inform flexible duration, support capacity, and facilitated discussion for newcomers.

The November 18 revision follows the organizer's clarification: longer editions combine expert-led presentations or panels, demos, demo workshops, and roundtable discussions; shorter editions use keynotes and lightning talks. Ryan Hsieh's Discovery Track and Implementation Track division is retained to guide the content mix for different levels of experience. Prescribed agendas and additional duration labels from the first draft are removed.

[Amsterdam](https://todogroup.org/blog/ospologylive-report/) and [Prague](https://todogroup.org/blog/2026-10-19-openchain-ospology-prague-2026-recap/) document the longer programme's practical and discussion formats. The organizer also identified China as a shorter example. The [Shanghai recap](https://todogroup.org/blog/2026-09-19-ospology-ospo-summit-china-2026-recap/) supplies event context, but does not establish a complete timed agenda; the shorter format follows the organizer's description.

The ten supplied toolkit files replace the newly drafted forms, fixed agendas, host brief, sponsorship call, and print layout. Their contents are preserved unchanged. A separate sponsorship guide documents the organizer's guidance on support for standalone events and the parent conference process for LF co-located events and tracks. Additional advocacy materials discussed in #749 remain future work.

Review still needs to confirm regional lead eligibility, application of the existing organizer vote, and replacing the former Europe-only frequency and location restrictions. Existing local edits on trusted meetup leadership and joint community organizing are retained. This draft does not record Steering Committee approval.

The former `ospology-live/` folder is consolidated under `events/`: edition records and historical documents are in `records/`, reusable session guides are in `guides/`, and the duplicate framework page is removed. The OSPOlogy Events index lists all eight archived editions. Repository links are updated to their new locations.
