# OSPOlogy sponsorship guide

## Standalone events

OSPOlogy events organized outside Linux Foundation conferences can accept financial or in-kind sponsorship to help cover delivery costs and attendee materials. Interested organizations can support:

- Event logistics, equipment, and AV services.
- Recording, photography, and media production, following the session's permission and recording arrangements.
- Lunch, food, beverages, and refreshments.
- Printed materials, attendee supplies, and swag.
- Other event costs agreed with the organizing team.

Organizations interested in sponsoring should contact the organizing team listed on the event's OCG page, indicating what they would like to support and whether the contribution is financial or in kind.

## Handling a contribution

1. Identify the cost or material to be covered, the amount or specification, and the delivery deadline.
2. Confirm the arrangement with the organizing team and relevant LF or regional contact, including who will handle payment, purchasing, or delivery.
3. Agree sponsor acknowledgement and any use of logos before publishing it on the event page or materials.
4. Record the confirmed contribution and responsible organizer in the event plan. Describe sponsored meals or materials accurately in attendee information.

Keep the programme vendor-neutral. Sponsorship does not confer control over session content or discussion outcomes. Sponsored recording must follow the framework's participation rules; protected roundtable discussions are not recorded.

## Events within Linux Foundation conferences

OSPOlogy events held within or co-located with Linux Foundation conferences, including KubeCon and PyTorch Conference, follow the parent event's official co-located event or event-track process and sponsorship requirements.

Coordinate opportunities, pricing, sponsor recognition, and commitments through the parent event's team. Confirm its requirements before offering sponsorship; standalone arrangements do not automatically apply to these events.
