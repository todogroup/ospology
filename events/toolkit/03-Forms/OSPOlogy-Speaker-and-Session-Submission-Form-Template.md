## Suggested fields
- Speaker name and preferred display name
- Contact email (organizer use)
- Organization and role
- Short biography
- Session title and abstract
- Session format and duration
- Audience and prerequisite knowledge
- Co-speakers or moderators
- Headshot upload and image credit
- Accessibility or AV needs (optional; organizer access only)
- Permission to use the supplied biography and headshot for this event, with organizer-approved wording

## Organizer setup
Define required fields, access, retention and a privacy notice before publishing. Keep contact information and accessibility requests out of the public programme. This is a question outline, not a live form.
