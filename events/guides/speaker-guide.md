# OSPOlogy speaker guide

Confirm the session's audience, language, duration, format, and intended outcome with the program team. Use practical examples relevant to the community and keep presentations vendor-neutral.

## Logistics

Ask the edition's host to confirm room layout, microphones, display connections, adapters, internet access, accessibility needs, and any remote participation arrangements. Bring required equipment and agree a time to test it. Arrival times, seating, and AV provision vary by venue.

## Materials and publication

Use your own slides or include the [OSPOlogy artwork](https://github.com/todogroup/artwork#ospology-logos) with appropriate attribution and agreed brand use. Agree a submission deadline and sharing route with organizers. Link approved slides from the current edition's record rather than an unrelated past event.

Confirm whether your presentation is public and whether you consent to recording. Organizers must tell attendees which sessions are recorded and keep recordings separate from protected roundtable and peer discussions. Share only content you have permission to publish; agree any third-party exceptions before materials enter the repository.

See the [shared participation principles](../framework.md#participation-and-follow-up) and [roundtable guide](roundtable-leader-guide.md) if your session includes peer discussion.
