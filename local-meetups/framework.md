# Community-led OSPOlogy meetups

Community-led meetups are part of the shared [OSPOlogy framework](../events/framework.md). They bring practitioners together in person, online, or through hybrid gatherings, with topics and languages chosen around local needs.

## The framework

See [one brand, two ways to participate](../events/framework.md#events-and-meetups) for the distinction between official events and community-led meetups.

## Meetup expectations

Use the framework's [shared participation principles](../events/framework.md#participation-and-follow-up): vendor neutrality, accessible participation, applicable policies, and protected peer discussion. Meetups are not sales events.

## Local ambassador requirements and responsibilities

See the [meetup organizer guide](local-ambassadors-guidelines.md#requirements-and-responsibilities). Organizing a meetup and appointment as a TODO ambassador are distinct responsibilities.

## How can I start a new OSPO Local Meetup?

Follow [start a community-led meetup](../events/framework.md#community-led-meetups). Find existing groups and listings on [OCG](https://ocgroups.dev/todo).

## How does TODO Group provide support?

Coordinate group setup, organizer access, policies, branding, and promotion with the TODO contact. Confirm specific support before making commitments; local organizers own delivery and follow-up.
